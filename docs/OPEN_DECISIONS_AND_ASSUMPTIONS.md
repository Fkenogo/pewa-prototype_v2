# Deliverable G — Open Decisions & Assumptions Log

**Consolidated Register of Strategic Decisions Reserved for Founder Determination**

---

## 1. Overview

In accordance with Section 10 and 12 of the Master Builder Brief, this register records substantive decision gaps and baseline assumptions. It explicitly categorizes each item so that product, commercial, technical, legal, and experience choices are evaluated with their corresponding trade-offs.

---
